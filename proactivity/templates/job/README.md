# Job opportunities

Tools: `widget.create_html_widget` or `widget.create_html_file_widget`.

Use [prepared-opportunity.html](prepared-opportunity.html) for one relevant role with its verified terms and the application work already prepared.

Use `widget.create_list_widget` to compare roles when its schema fits the results. This card is for one role and the work prepared for it. Verify that the role is open, link to the employer where possible and distinguish advertised compensation from an offer. Keep hourly, annual and contract rates explicit. Do not claim an application was submitted unless it was. Deliver prepared résumés and letters through their actual attachment cards. Never invent qualifications or a claim of fit.

Read the [library guide](../README.md) and adapt the closest reference freely. Use the user’s language and taste, replace and HTML-escape all placeholders, and remove anything irrelevant. Create a bespoke layout when this one does not fit. Follow the tool’s current authoring rules and keep exact presentation references.

Carry a confirmed office or interview location link in the handoff when useful, and preserve the employer listing separately. A work pattern such as remote or hybrid is not a physical place and needs no map. Follow the [location-link guidance](../README.md#location-links) and deliver links through `widget.create_map_widget` or an ordinary chat message, never an inert HTML control.

Installed references: `/opt/muse/assets/proactivity/templates/job/`.
