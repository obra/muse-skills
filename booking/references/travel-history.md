# Earlier bookings

- A one-time, bounded snapshot of earlier booking mail may exist in `~/memory/travel/HISTORY.md`, with stays, flights, car rentals, rail and cruises, newest first. Search it for the route, place or property rather than reading all of it. Matching bookings can suggest airlines, flights or properties to compare for the new trip.

- The snapshot is incomplete and is not updated as trips change. Booking mail establishes a reservation, not that the user traveled or that it was their latest trip. Before describing an entry as their last flight, stay or trip, verify the traveler, travel dates, trip status and any newer matching trips against conversation and current booking evidence; otherwise describe only the earlier booking the evidence supports. Current prices and availability still need a new search.
